The House

A 32 × 24 m block house with four rooms, a walkable roof and a wide front terrace. Step through the south door, or pick a room from the Dock.

Visit the poolEast shore

一栋 32 × 24 米的方块大房子：四个房间、可上人的屋顶和一整排门前台。从南门走进来，或者在 Dock 上直接选一个房间。

去游泳池去东岸

Pool

A 12 × 8 m swimmable pool with a tiled basin, underwater lamps, a ladder, two loungers and a parasol. Walk into the water to swim; F opens this card.

Head inside

12 × 8 米的真泳池：瓷砖池底、水下灯、扶梯、两张躺椅和一把遮阳伞。走进水里就能游泳，按 F 打开这张卡片。

进屋看看

Bedroom

A quiet room behind the north partition: a linen bed, two bedside lamps and a full-height wardrobe. A doorway at the back connects to the study.

Next: study

北侧隔墙后的安静卧室：亚麻床铺、两盏床头灯和一整面衣柜。房间后面留了一道门，可以直接走到书房。

下一站：书房

Study

A working room with a 4 m desk, a lit monitor, a desk lamp, five bookshelves along the north wall and a reading corner by the window.

Back to the house

可以干活的书房：4 米长书桌、亮着的屏幕、台灯、北墙一整排五组书架，窗边还有一个阅读角。

回到大房子

Billiards

The main piece on the living floor: a 4.4 m green table with a racked set of balls, plus a cue rack, a bar counter and three pendant lamps.

Up to the bedroom

起居室的主角：4.4 米绿色球台，摆好了一组彩球，旁边是球杆架、小吧台和三盏吊灯。

上楼去卧室

Little Island

A small sand islet in the east lagoon: two palms, a striped parasol, a bench, a stack of crates and a beacon on the shore. Cross the plank bridge to get there, or press F near the sand.

Back to the bridge

东侧潟湖里的一座沙洲小岛：两棵棕榈、一把条纹遮阳伞、一条长椅、几只木箱，岸上还有一盏小灯。走木板桥过去，或者站在沙地上按 F。

回到桥梁

The Bridge

A 6 m plank bridge over the lagoon, with wooden railings on both sides and two lamp posts at the ends. The deck is real ground, so you can walk across it.

On to the bus stop

潟湖上一座 6 米长的木板桥：两侧木栏杆，桥头各一盏灯。桥面是真地面，可以直接走过去。

去公交站

Bus Stop

A covered stop on the east road: four steel posts, a blue canopy, a glazed windbreak, a lit timetable board, a bench, a route sign and a ceiling light.

Board the bus

东侧路边的一座有顶公交站：四根钢柱、蓝色雨棚、玻璃挡风墙、发光的时刻表、长椅、站牌和一盏顶灯。

看看公交车

The Bus

A 12 m yellow city bus parked on the paved road: six wheels, side windows, a windscreen, headlights, tail lights, a route display and a blue door on the south side.

Back to the house

停在铺装路上的 12 米黄色城市公交车：六个车轮、两侧车窗、前挡风玻璃、前后车灯、路线牌，南侧还有一扇蓝色车门。
